About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2015 with one oven, one sourdough starter, and a simple idea: our neighborhood deserved real bread, made by hand, close to home. We named it after the North Star because we want to be something our community can count on every morning.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

  • Local flour: stone milled grain from regional family farms.
  • Seasonal fruit: our pastries change with what nearby orchards harvest.
  • Real ingredients: real butter and eggs, no artificial flavors.
  • Less waste: day old bread is donated to local food pantries.
